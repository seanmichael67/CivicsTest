Official USCIS 2025 question 57 of 128

Who is the Chief Justice of the United States now?

Accepted answer

  • Visit uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates for the name of the Chief Justice of the United States.

Give this answer aloud when the officer asks. The civics test is spoken, not written.

This answer changes. It depends on who currently holds the office or on where you live, so it is not fixed like the historical questions. Verify the current answer at uscis.gov before your interview.
