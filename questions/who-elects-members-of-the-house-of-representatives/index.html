Official USCIS 2025 question 34 of 128

Who elects members of the House of Representatives?

Accepted answer

  • Citizens from their (congressional) district

Give this answer aloud when the officer asks. The civics test is spoken, not written.

Practice saying this out loud

The civics test is administered orally — an officer reads a question and you answer by speaking. Practising by tapping multiple-choice buttons trains the wrong skill.
