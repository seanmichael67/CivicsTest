All 128 USCIS civics test questions

These are the official questions for the 2025 version of the naturalization civics test, which applies to anyone who filed Form N-400 on or after 20 October 2025. Applicants who filed before that date take the older 100-question version.

You are asked up to 20 of these and need 12 correct to pass. Questions marked ★ are on the 65/20 list.
