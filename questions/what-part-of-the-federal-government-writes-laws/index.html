Official USCIS 2025 question 18 of 128

What part of the federal government writes laws?

Accepted answers

  • (U.S.) Congress
  • (U.S. or national) legislature
  • Legislative branch

Any one of these 3 answers is accepted. You do not need to give all of them — the officer is listening for one correct response.

Practice saying this out loud

The civics test is administered orally — an officer reads a question and you answer by speaking. Practising by tapping multiple-choice buttons trains the wrong skill.
