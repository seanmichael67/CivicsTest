Official USCIS 2025 question 98 of 128

When did all men get the right to vote?

Accepted answers

  • After the Civil War
  • During Reconstruction
  • (With the) 15th Amendment
  • 1870

Any one of these 4 answers is accepted. You do not need to give all of them — the officer is listening for one correct response.

Practice saying this out loud

The civics test is administered orally — an officer reads a question and you answer by speaking. Practising by tapping multiple-choice buttons trains the wrong skill.
