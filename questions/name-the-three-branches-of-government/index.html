Official USCIS 2025 question 16 of 128

Name the three branches of government.

Accepted answers

  • Legislative, executive, and judicial
  • Congress, president, and the courts

Any one of these 2 answers is accepted. You do not need to give all of them — the officer is listening for one correct response.

Practice saying this out loud

The civics test is administered orally — an officer reads a question and you answer by speaking. Practising by tapping multiple-choice buttons trains the wrong skill.
