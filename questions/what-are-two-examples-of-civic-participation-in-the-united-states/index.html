Official USCIS 2025 question 69 of 128

What are two examples of civic participation in the United States?

Accepted answers

  • Vote
  • Run for office
  • Join a political party
  • Help with a campaign
  • Join a civic group
  • Join a community group
  • Give an elected official your opinion (on an issue)
  • Contact elected officials
  • Support or oppose an issue or policy
  • Write to a newspaper

Any one of these 10 answers is accepted. You do not need to give all of them — the officer is listening for one correct response.

Practice saying this out loud

The civics test is administered orally — an officer reads a question and you answer by speaking. Practising by tapping multiple-choice buttons trains the wrong skill.
